# Fact-check: family portraits, batch 4 (2026-09-28)

Scope: 13 family portraits (psophiidae … trochilidae) written before the species cards, the card fact-check and the
eBird/Clements 2025 update. Method: `.claude/skills/fact-check/SKILL.md`. Counts recomputed from `data/species_index.json`
(field `family`, the same number as `data/families.json` `species_count`, which the page header shows); every species
named at a site checked against `data/site_species.json` (`sure`/`maybe`), targets against `data/sites_resolved.json`,
days against `data/itinerary.json`; facts against `data/texts/<slug>.json` (Wikipedia en/es/ru, CC BY-SA 4.0), web only
where noted. Portrait schema (content/families/README.md) has no `sources:` field, so sources are listed here only.
Edits are ru + en together. Wording pattern for list targets that eBird does not support at the named site: keep the
species as a listed target, say that records there are few, and point to the sites where it is `sure`/`maybe`.

- `data/species_index.json`: `ru: null` for Ecuadorian Rail (`rallus-aequatorialis`, post-split slug), Golden-bellied Starfrontlet (`coeligena-bonapartei`) and Pink-billed Cnemoscopus (`cnemoscopus-rubrirostris`).

Needs a mapping in `pipeline/mappings/aco_to_ebird.json` / clements2025.json (verify against the Clements 2025 checklist before remapping).
- Check whether eBird/Clements 2025 splits Giant Hummingbird (`patagona-gigas`, target at La Cocha) into Southern/Northern Giant Hummingbird (P. chaski for the Andean population, Williamson et al. 2024); not verified here.
- `data/sites_resolved.json` `target_species` lists many birds that eBird rates `unlikely` or not at all at that site (e.g. Black-billed Mountain-Toucan at Trampolín/Sibundoy, Subtropical Pygmy-Owl at Trampolín, Riparian Antbird at Puerto Asís, Black Tinamou at Isla Escondida, Nazca Booby at Playa del Morro, Glossy Ibis at Maragrícola, Scarlet-breasted Dacnis at Río Ñambí, Blue-browed Tanager at Trampolín). - Family counts include `hypothetical` species (Thamnophilidae 119 incl. 1, Ramphastidae 21 incl. 1, Tinamidae 19 incl. 2, Thraupidae 180 incl. 2, Trochilidae 166 incl. 1). Portraits follow the data count shown on the page; decide whether headers/portraits should exclude hypotheticals.
