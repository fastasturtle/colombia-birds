# Fact-check of family portraits, part 2 (2026-09-28)

Scope: 13 family portraits in `content/families/`: cuculidae, donacobiidae, eurypygidae, falconidae, formicariidae,
fregatidae, fringillidae, furnariidae, galbulidae, grallariidae, hydrobatidae, icteridae, laridae.

Method: species counts were recomputed from `data/species_index.json` (field `family`) and checked against
`data/families.json` `species_count`. Every species named at a site was checked against `data/site_species.json`
(sure/maybe/unlikely), `target_species` in `data/sites_resolved.json` and the day numbers in `data/itinerary.json`.
Field marks and facts were checked against `data/texts/<slug>.json` (Wikipedia extracts, CC BY-SA 4.0) and the
checked species cards in `content/species/`. Web lookups were used only for two facts the extracts could not settle.
The portrait schema (`content/families/README.md`) has no `sources:` field, so sources are listed here only.
Each file was edited in Russian and English together.

Common sources: project data (`data/species_index.json`, `data/families.json`, `data/site_species.json`,
`data/sites_resolved.json`, `data/itinerary.json`, `data/sites.json`); Wikipedia extracts in `data/texts/`.

## formicariidae

Claims: 16 · corrected: 4 · removed: 1 · unverifiable: 0
- ❌ Short-tailed Antthrush «поёт на Трамплине птиц» and Black-faced Antthrush «в Исла-Эскондиде» were removed. Site_species has no record at Trampolín and rates both `unlikely` at Isla Escondida. The text now says both are unlikely in the Amazonian foothills and adds Black-capped Antthrush (maybe at Río Ñambí, Bangsias and Aves y Flórez, days 16–18). The Rufous-breasted site list was corrected to Río Ñambí, Aves y Flórez and La Nutria (days 16–19).
- ✏️ `fact`: the eponym is corrected. The species is named after Paul A. Schwartz because he was the first to notice how different its song is from that of Rufous-tailed Antthrush; it was recognised as a separate species in 1992 after its songs were analysed. The old wording said he was «one of the first to record Venezuela's bird voices systematically». Source: Wikipedia «Schwartz's antthrush» (data/texts/chamaeza-turdina.json, Taxonomy).
- ✏️ Confusable with rallidae: «живёт в сухом лесу» contradicted the body text (humid forest) and was changed to «ходит по подстилке леса вдали от воды».
- ✏️ Formicarius range: «в низинах и предгорьях» changed to «в основном…», because Rufous-breasted Antthrush lives at 850–2 400 m. Source: species_index.

## galbulidae

Claims: 18 · corrected: 3 · removed: 1 · unverifiable: 0
- ❌ Coppery-chested Jacamar: «медно-рыжая грудь» was wrong and is corrected. The breast is glossy green, the belly dark rufous and the bare eye-ring orange. Source: Wikipedia «Coppery-chested jacamar» (Description); card galbula-pastazae.
- ✏️ `route_note`: Coppery-chested Jacamar at Finca Discosura and Trampolín changed. It is `unlikely` at Discosura and has no data at Trampolín, and in Colombia it is known only from a single site near the Ecuador border. It is now «цель Финки Дискосура, встреча маловероятна», and Trampolín was dropped. Great Jacamar «бывает на платформах» became «отмечают редко» (unlikely). White-eared and White-chinned are maybe at Playa Rica and Puerto Asís. Source: site_species; Wikipedia (Distribution).
- ✏️ «в Андах выше 1 500 м их нет» changed to «выше 1 600 м практически нет». Source: the Colombian record of Coppery-chested Jacamar is at 1 600 m (Wikipedia).
- ✅ Count of 13 matches the data. The butterfly-palatability experiments with Rufous-tailed Jacamar are well known (Chai 1986) and are kept.

## icteridae

Claims: 22 · corrected: 3 · removed: 0 · unverifiable: 0
- ✏️ Peruvian Meadowlark: the days changed from 20–21 to 19–21, Finca Maragrícola was added and it is phrased as «возможен» (maybe at all three coastal sites).
- ✏️ Body: «лойки с красной грудью … в горных долинах» was wrong, because the highland meadowlark near Bogotá is the yellow-breasted Eastern Meadowlark. The meadowlark sentence was rewritten to say this. Source: species_index (Eastern Meadowlark at Bogotá sites; Leistes are lowland species).
- ✏️ «Трупиаловые ходят по земле шагом» was limited to ground-feeding species, because oropendolas and caciques live in trees.
- ✅ Count of 41 matches the data. Red-bellied Grackle is endemic and maybe at El Encanto, La Drymophila and Trampolín. The oropendolas are sure at Isla Escondida and Puerto Asís, Scarlet-rumped Cacique is sure at the Chocó lodges, and Giant Cowbird parasitises oropendolas and caciques.

## laridae

Claims: 16 · corrected: 2 · removed: 0 · unverifiable: 0
- ✏️ Inca Tern «нерегулярный гость с юга; ищи на скалистом островке» (`route_note`) and «доходит до Колумбии лишь изредка» (body) now say it is a vagrant in Colombia with a handful of mostly undocumented records. ## Data issues

- `data/families.json`: `names.ru` is null for donaco1 (Donacobiidae; the portrait uses «Донакобиевые») and for oceani2 (Oceanitidae).
- `data/species_index.json`: `ru` is null for Common Squirrel-Cuckoo (piaya-cayana), Black-capped Antthrush (formicarius-nigricapillus), Pacific Hornero (furnarius-cinnamomeus; its IUCN is also null), and several antpittas (Boyaca, Tawny, Equatorial, Muisca-group splits: grallaria-alticola, -quitensis, -saturata, -alvarezi, -spatiator, -saltuensis).
- `data/site_species.json`: lowland species show up as «maybe» at high Bogotá sites, probably because of the search radius or a hotspot mix. Examples: Greater Ani (0–500 m) at jardin-botanico-bogota (n=916) and chicaque; Rufous-tailed Jacamar at chicaque (n=407); Carib Grackle and Yellow Oriole at chicaque and jardin-botanico-bogota. This is worth a look in the pipeline.
- `data/species_index.json`: Coppery-chested Jacamar (galbula-pastazae) is a target at finca-discosura, but in Colombia it is known from a single Nariño site. The target itself may be doubtful.
- `data/species_index.json`: Buckley's Forest-Falcon and Cacicus/Galbula entries have `elev` with the string «L» (e.g. ['L', 1800]), so they cannot be compared numerically.
