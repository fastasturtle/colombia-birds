## Data issues

- **Site targets vs eBird (data/sites.json `target_species_raw` vs data/site_species.json):** Great Potoo is a target at El Escondite but has no rating there and is `unlikely` elsewhere in Putumayo. Ladder-tailed Nightjar is a target at Playa Rica (`unlikely`), and Choco Poorwill is a target at Río Ñambí (`unlikely` at all Chocó sites). Nocturnal species are underreported in eBird, so the `unlikely` state may understate them. Worth a note in docs/TODO.md: consider a state for "trip-report target, few eBird records".
- **Choco Tyrannulet (zimmerius-albigularis)** is a focus species but has no entry for any site in data/site_species.json, although Golden-faced Tyrannulet (Z. chrysops) is `maybe` at Bangsias. This may be a mapping issue after the albigularis/chrysops split (check pipeline/mappings/clements2025.json and the eBird taxon used for the site bar charts).
